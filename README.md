### Sales Performance Dashboard: Analyzing the Impact of Media Advertising on Sales

Developed an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard using SQL and Tableau to analyze the relationship between advertising expenditure and sales performance across three major media channels: TV, Radio, and Newspaper. The primary objective of this project was to understand how advertising investments relate to sales revenue, evaluate the efficiency of different marketing channels, and generate meaningful insights to support data-driven business decisions.

The project involved working with an advertising dataset containing media spending and sales information. SQL was used to clean, process, organize, aggregate, and analyze the data to prepare it for visualization. Various SQL queries were executed to calculate total and average advertising expenditure, summarize sales performance, compare advertising investments with sales revenue, and evaluate the relationship between individual advertising channels and sales outcomes.

Tableau was used to develop interactive dashboards and visualizations that present key performance indicators and business insights in an easy-to-understand format. The dashboard includes a media spend breakdown to compare advertising expenditure across TV, Radio, and Newspaper, along with sales performance visualizations to examine the relationship between advertising investments and generated sales. Scatter plots were used to explore the association between advertising spend and sales, while bar charts and other visualizations helped compare channel-level performance.

The project also incorporated ROI analysis to compare sales relative to advertising expenditure across different media channels. Historical sales and advertising data were examined to identify patterns and explore potential sales trends. These analyses help stakeholders understand how advertising budgets are distributed, which channels demonstrate stronger relationships with sales, and where further investigation into marketing effectiveness may be valuable.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of advertising expenditure and sales performance, making it easier to interpret complex data, identify patterns, and communicate findings through visual reports. The insights can support marketing campaign evaluation, advertising budget planning, and strategic decision-making. However, the analysis is based on historical data, and the observed relationships do not independently establish that advertising expenditure caused changes in sales.

Overall, this project demonstrates practical skills in SQL-based data analysis, data preparation, Tableau dashboard development, data visualization, ROI analysis, and business intelligence. It highlights the ability to transform raw datasets into structured reports and meaningful insights that help businesses better understand marketing performance and make informed decisions.
